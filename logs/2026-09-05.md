## Decisions made and why

- **Did not treat 0/75 replies over 5 days as a failure signal requiring a full pivot**:
  ~9.3% of the 75 sends are confirmed-invalid addresses (bounces), shrinking the effective
  delivered sample to ~68. More importantly, most of these addresses are Google-Maps-listing
  inboxes that small business owners in India often check irregularly — sometimes only
  weekly, sometimes barely at all if the listing email isn't their primary one. Cold-email
  response data for this segment typically takes 1-4 weeks to materialize meaningfully. 5
  days is still an early sample, not a verdict.
- **Did make one low-risk experimental change**: rotated in 2 new subject-line variants
  ("{Business} — kitni {noun} enquiries chhoot rahi hain?" and "{Business} ke liye 48-ghante
  free pilot — {noun} follow-up automate") for tonight's 14 sends, alternating between them,
  rather than reusing the single "Chhoti si baat" subject used in all 75 prior sends. This
  gives us subject-line comparison data once replies start arriving, without touching the
  compliant body content, targeting approach, or send cadence. A middle path between "ignore
  the flat zero-reply signal" and "throw out a working process on a small sample."
- **Set an explicit re-escalation checkpoint** (night ~10-12, roughly 2026-09-10 to
  2026-09-12) rather than leaving "reassess if still zero" open-ended and vague, which is
  what led to it drifting from night 5's flag to now without a firm trigger. If still zero
  replies by then, the next session should escalate to `STATE.md` → "Needs owner decision"
  with a concrete recommendation (try phone/WhatsApp, or rework offer/targeting) instead of
  continuing to grind out research passes unchanged.
- **Sent all 14 from pass 6 rather than holding some back**: the whole verified list was
  under the 15/night cap, so no reason not to use all of it (consistent with prior nights'
  approach when a research pass came in under 15).
