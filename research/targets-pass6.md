**Sourcing method:** WebSearch only. Every email listed below was seen as literal,
visible text in a search-result snippet pulled from the business's own website, a
Facebook page, or a public directory listing (JustDial, IndiaMART, Sulekha, etc.) —
never guessed. Every candidate email was put through a **second, independent
quoted-string search** (searching for the exact address in quotes) before being
accepted, the same discipline passes 2-5 used. A large number of initially-surfaced
leads failed that second pass — appearing once in a directory AI-summary but never
reconfirming independently — and were moved to the phone/form-only table or dropped
entirely rather than risk a wrong-recipient send. Notable drops and checks:

- **Kadapa retry:** per the pass-5 note, tried different specific-business queries this
  time (dental clinics, interior designers, footwear/mobile shops) instead of repeating
  the gym/boutique/saree-retail/coaching searches that only found phone-only listings
  last pass. It worked for one category — **G Interiors** (Hafele-authorised modular
  kitchen/interior studio) reconfirmed cleanly. A Kadapa dental clinic
  (Swetha Dental Clinic) and a footwear/mobile-accessories shop in Onipenta both
  surfaced an email once but failed the second independent check (the footwear shop
  listing also never surfaced a business name, only a Sulekha category page) — dropped,
  not even phone-only since no verified name/phone pairing was found.
- **Kollam:** avoided yoga-studio searches entirely per instructions (Art of Living
  exclusion). Two verified emails found in boutique/driving-school categories instead

  photography studio actually pointed to a Kolkata-branded Facebook page

  mis-tagging rather than a genuine Kollam business.
- **Chain/franchise exclusions:** **Green Trends Salon (Proddatur)** is a well-known

  Kolhapur exclusion logic. **Mahaveer Driving School (Cuttack)** looked promising
