# LeadSetu — Candidate Target Businesses (Research Pass 5, 2026-09-04)

**Purpose:** Top-up batch (pass 5) of candidate small Indian businesses for LeadSetu's
outreach, sourced after research passes 1-4 (`targets.md`, `targets-pass2.md`,
`targets-pass3.md`, `targets-pass4.md`) were fully exhausted or reserved. Same target
profile and sourcing rules as prior passes. Cities were deliberately chosen to NOT
overlap any prior pass: passes 1-4 together covered Indore, Nagpur, Coimbatore, Jaipur,
Lucknow, Surat, Bhopal, Nashik, Vadodara, Kanpur, Dehradun, Mysore, Guwahati, Ranchi,
Bhubaneswar, Kochi, Amritsar, Vijayawada, Patna, Raipur, Jabalpur, Thiruvananthapuram,
Trichy, Udaipur, Rajkot, Varanasi, Chandigarh, Dhanbad, Jodhpur, Agra, Ludhiana,
Jalandhar, Aurangabad, Bareilly, Siliguri, Gwalior, Amravati, Warangal, Salem, Madurai,
Hisar, Meerut, Rohtak, Belagavi, Ajmer, Durgapur, Moradabad, Tirupur, Panipat, Bathinda,
and Karimnagar. This pass covers Muzaffarpur, Bikaner, Anantapur, Gorakhpur, Nellore, and
Solapur.

**Sourcing method:** WebSearch only. Every email listed below was seen as literal,
visible text in a search-result snippet pulled from the business's own website, a
Facebook page, or a public directory listing (JustDial, IndiaMART, Sulekha, etc.) —
never guessed. Every candidate email was put through a **second, independent
quoted-string search** (searching for the exact address in quotes) before being
accepted, the same discipline passes 2-4 used. A large number of initially-surfaced
leads (roughly a dozen) failed that second pass — appearing once in a directory
AI-summary but never reconfirming independently — and were dropped or moved to the
phone/form-only table rather than risk a wrong-recipient send. Notable drops and checks:

- **Dr Deepika Skin & Hair Clinic (Bikaner)** — the second search also surfaced an
  unrelated "Dr. Deepika" skin clinic in Ludhiana. Checked explicitly: different doctor,
  different practice, no shared branding or ownership — coincidence of a common name, not
  a chain. Kept.
- **Teja Academy (Anantapur)** — checked explicitly for multi-city branches (pass 4
  dropped a similarly-named KS Academy for exactly this reason). Confirmed single-location
  (Kamalanagar, Anantapur) coaching institute with no other-city presence found. Kept.
- **Chinni Pet Store (Nellore)** — has two branches, but both are within Nellore city
  itself (main store + a newer "Branch 2" for grooming), not a multi-city chain. Kept as a
  single small local operator, not excluded.
- No "spa"-branded businesses surfaced this pass that needed the Kota/Tirupur-style
  adult-services brand-safety check; all salon/wellness listings found had ordinary
  beauty-parlour service menus.
- **RK Studio (Bilaspur, CG)**, **Elegant Designs (Korba)**, **Gaya Driving School
  (Gaya)**, **Dr. Gaur's Pet Planet (Bikaner)**, **Ameyaa Studio (Gorakhpur)**, and
  **Medhini's Boutique (Nellore)** each had an email surface once from a directory/social
  summary but it did not reconfirm on the second independent search — all six moved to
  the phone/form-only table below instead of being emailed.
- Also explored but dropped with no phone captured either (not even phone-only): Samarpan
  Coaching Institute (Sikar), AB Interiors (Solapur), R.D. Lomate Driving School
  (Solapur), Varalakshmiboutique and Dharmavaram Silk Sarees (Anantapur), Indu's Yoga
  Classes (Bikaner). Kadapa was explored across four categories (gym, boutique, saree
  retail, coaching) and yielded only phone-only directory listings, no business-branded
  email — worth another pass with different specific-business queries. Art of Living
  centers came up repeatedly for "yoga studio" searches in Ichalkaranji, Kollam,
  Gorakhpur, and Solapur — all skipped as a large multi-city spiritual-movement franchise,
  consistent with the pass-4 Kolhapur exclusion.
