# LeadSetu — Candidate Target Businesses (Research Pass 4, 2026-09-03)

**Purpose:** Small top-up batch (pass 4) of candidate small Indian businesses for
LeadSetu's outreach, sourced after research passes 1-3 (`targets.md`,
`targets-pass2.md`, `targets-pass3.md`) were fully exhausted or reserved. Same target
profile and sourcing rules as prior passes — see those files for the full method
description. Cities were deliberately chosen to NOT overlap any prior pass: passes 1-3
together covered Indore, Nagpur, Coimbatore, Jaipur, Lucknow, Surat, Bhopal, Nashik,
Vadodara, Kanpur, Dehradun, Mysore, Guwahati, Ranchi, Bhubaneswar, Kochi, Amritsar,
Vijayawada, Patna, Raipur, Jabalpur, Thiruvananthapuram, Trichy, Udaipur, Rajkot,
Varanasi, Chandigarh, Dhanbad, Jodhpur, Agra, Ludhiana, Jalandhar, Aurangabad, Bareilly,
Siliguri, Gwalior, Amravati, Warangal, Salem, Madurai, Hisar, Meerut, and Rohtak. This
pass covers Belagavi (Belgaum), Ajmer, Durgapur, Moradabad, Tirupur, Panipat, Bathinda,
and Karimnagar.

**Sourcing method:** WebSearch only. Every email listed below was seen as literal,
visible text in a search-result snippet pulled from the business's own website, a
Facebook page, or a public directory listing (JustDial, Sulekha, etc.) — never guessed.
Every candidate email was put through a **second, independent quoted-string search**
(searching for the exact address in quotes) before being accepted, the same discipline
passes 2-3 used. Several initially-surfaced leads failed that second pass or tripped
another compliance rule and were dropped rather than risk a wrong-recipient send:

- **KS Academy (Guntur)** — `ksacademy23@gmail.com` reconfirmed on the second search, but
  the same address is the shared contact for KS Academy branches in Bengaluru, Hyderabad,
  Kolkata, Ernakulam, Coimbatore, and Tambaram — excluded as a multi-city chain, not a
  solo/small operator.
- **Amrit Photography (Bathinda)** and **Maalaxmi Driving School (Karimnagar)** — each
  email surfaced once (from a Justdial/Facebook-derived summary) but did not reappear on
  a second, independent quoted-string search, so both were dropped and the businesses
  moved to the phone/form-only table instead of risking a wrong-recipient send.
- **Ganga Designers (Moradabad)** — the `gangadesigners@gmail.com` address did not
  reconfirm on a second search either, and no separate phone number could be verified for
  the same business, so it was dropped entirely (not even added to the phone-only table).
- **Ayana Wellness Spa & Salon (Tirupur)** — flagged for a closer look given the "spa"
  naming (pass 3 excluded a Kota spa cluster for adult-services language). Checked its
  listed services directly: standard day-spa/salon menu (Western massage, steam bath,
  pedicure/manicure, facials, haircuts, bridal facial/makeup packages) with no
  body-to-body/"extra services" language anywhere — kept as a legitimate wellness salon.
- Nizamabad (real estate), Cuttack (event planning/catering), Solapur (tuition coaching),
  and Hubli/Hubballi (pet grooming) were all explored but yielded no business-branded
  email that could be sourced as visible text — only phone-only directory listings or
  emails belonging to unrelated/regional-chain entities (e.g. a Cuttack "event planner"
  operating across Bhubaneswar and Cuttack). No candidates added from these cities this
  pass; worth trying again with different specific-business queries.

**Status:** 9 businesses total. **7 have a verified, publicly-visible, business-branded
email address**, all double-checked as described above. 2 have phone/form only (see
bottom table) — future phone/WhatsApp outreach candidates, do not guess an email for
them. **3 of the 7 emailed 2026-09-03 (Tulsi Dental Clinic, Mission Fitness Multi Gym,
Mittal Sarees — picked for city/category spread). 4 remain unused: Dr. Netra's Laser
Skin Clinic (Belagavi), Ayana Wellness Spa & Salon (Tirupur), Fitness Unplugged
