# LeadSetu — Candidate Target Businesses (Research Pass 3, 2026-09-02)

**Purpose:** Third batch of candidate small Indian businesses for LeadSetu's outreach,
found after research passes 1 (`targets.md`) and 2 (`targets-pass2.md`) were fully
exhausted. Same target profile and sourcing rules as passes 1 and 2 — see those files
for the full method description. Cities were deliberately chosen to NOT overlap either
prior pass: pass 1 covered Indore, Nagpur, Coimbatore, Jaipur, Lucknow, Surat, Bhopal,
Nashik, Vadodara, Kanpur, Dehradun, Mysore, Guwahati, Ranchi, Bhubaneswar; pass 2 covered
Kochi, Amritsar, Vijayawada, Patna, Raipur, Jabalpur, Thiruvananthapuram, Trichy, Udaipur,
Rajkot, Varanasi, Chandigarh, Dhanbad, Jodhpur. This pass covers Agra, Ludhiana,
Jalandhar, Aurangabad (Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar), Bareilly, Siliguri, Gwalior, Amravati,
Warangal, Salem, Madurai, Hisar, Meerut, and Rohtak.

**Sourcing method:** WebSearch only (WebFetch to arbitrary external domains is blocked in
this sandbox). Every email listed below was seen as literal, visible text in a
search-result snippet pulled from the business's own website, a Facebook page, or a
public directory listing (JustDial, IndiaMART, etc.) — never guessed. Every candidate
email was put through a **second, independent quoted-string search** (searching for the
exact address in quotes) before being accepted, the same discipline pass 2 used. Several
initially-surfaced emails failed that second pass or tripped another compliance rule and
were dropped rather than risk a wrong-recipient send:

- **Alfa Furniture (Rohtak)** — the email traced back to Ruhani Industries Pvt Ltd, a
  multi-city office-furniture manufacturer (Panchkula HQ, showrooms in Chandigarh, Etawah,
  Prayagraj, etc.) — excluded as a chain, not a solo/small operator.
- **Dream Interiors (Rohtak)** and **Bridal Beauty Parlour (Meerut)** — the emails did not
  reappear on a second, independent quoted-string search, so they were dropped and the
  businesses moved to the phone/form-only table instead of risking a wrong-recipient send.
- **Murali Electronics (Nellore)** — same issue, email did not reconfirm independently.
- **Delight Spa / Vibe Health Spa / Royal Enclave Spa (Kota)** — dropped entirely. The
  same phone number and Gmail address recur across several differently-branded "spa"
  domains, and some of those domains' own listed service names (body-to-body, "massage
  girls," "extra services") read as an adult-services operation rather than the
  respectable wellness/salon business LeadSetu wants to be associated with.
- **Dr Bhatia Medical Coaching Institute (Vellore)** — excluded as a chain (Pvt Ltd
  company headquartered in Delhi with branches in Bangalore, Vellore, and elsewhere).
- Several personal-name-pattern Gmail addresses surfaced (e.g. `ranjula.jain@gmail.com`
  for a Meerut salon, `mohammadarshad1232@gmail.com` and `amanrobin86@gmail.com` for two
  Ludhiana salons, `chatvikash.singh@gmail.com` and `noton.mahato46@gmail.com` for two
  Jamshedpur gyms, `tanzeem.a.786@gmail.com` for a Bareilly parlour) — all excluded per the
  no-personal-email rule even where directly confirmed, since they read as an individual's
  private address rather than a business-branded one.

**Status:** 18 businesses total. **14 have a verified, publicly-visible, business-branded
email address**, all double-checked as described above. 4 have phone/form only (see
bottom table) — future phone/WhatsApp outreach candidates, do not guess an email for them.
**2 of the 14 emailed 2026-09-02 (used to top up night 3's cap to 15/15); remaining 12
emailed 2026-09-03 (night 4). All 14 verified-email candidates now contacted — this file
is fully exhausted for email outreach.**
